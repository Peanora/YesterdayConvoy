# Guerrilla order cut-in

- The illustration `Resources/GuerrillaOrder/approved-cut-in.png` is the exact, unmodified AI concept accepted by the user on 2026-11-06. Its original project-external filename was `guerrilla-fullscreen-v1.png`.
- SHA256: `8a5883dcb661468342e57c5a60713ef75004a16c0e07cfd5861d4f6510b73127`.
- The generated illustration depicts the project's Arknights-inspired guerrilla characters. It is a generated concept adaptation, not original-game portrait extraction, animation footage, or an independent grant of rights from the original characters' rightsholder. Existing character provenance and rights limitations remain in effect.
- On 2026-11-06 the user rejected the first poster-alpha implementation, reviewed a new actual V2 motion video, and approved V2 for integration. V2 uses a live nine-sector radial glass composition. Fixed slots are M15 (230 degrees), M16 (190), M03 (150), M20 (270), M19 (310), M18 (350), M17 (30), then two bottom extension sectors (70 and 110). Missing lineup members retain dark glass; deployed status does not control portraits. No fixed video is played by the game.
- The current runtime crops one face region per character from this unchanged concept atlas. Uniform, eye-anchored cover mapping fills each complete wedge without rectangular picture edges or another character's face/title. CPU geometry independently enters from screen edges between 0.08 and 0.475 seconds, impacts at 0.49, holds, then splits each sector into twelve individually translated/rotated/eroded textured triangles between 0.9 and 2.1 seconds. Crystal, crossed rays, outer glass particles and a native text caption continue a short tail until 2.6 seconds.
- This motion grammar was studied from the actual 145.8-147.8 second sequence of `https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1so1QBNEzs/`. Colors, masks, character crops and timings are this project's approved adaptation, not a frame-accurate reproduction or a claim of the original game's particle engineering. The external authoring sources were `guerrilla-v2/design/character-panes.js` and `guerrilla-v2/preview/motion.js`.
- The two bottom sectors now bind the user-approved preparation selection of at most two owned non-native guerrilla kinds. Their images reuse the existing CardDefinition.Portrait sprites, without generating or replacing character art. Selected members kept in reserve leave their assigned sector blank; battle tier and cut-in membership read the same locked snapshot. Manual designation is free during preparation; this does not add equipment drops or a generic emblem inventory.
- After the user's explicit follow-up on 2026-11-06, geometry and sound use a separate pause-aware cinematic clock: 1x and 2x combat retain the same cut-in duration and original sound pitch; the existing skill-menu slow motion remains. The model commits a real global volley at 0.9 cinematic seconds; the renderer only reads its actual per-defender HP-loss records, revealing the battlefield with a sweep and damage labels. The original follow-up sound resource is loaded as `GuerrillaOrder/followup-original` and starts once at the 0.9-second fracture cue; its independent acquisition/provenance is recorded with the audio delivery. This replaces the rejected first version's synthesized `shing`; the audio is not claimed to be original project synthesis or licensed by the atlas generator.
- The mesh shader accumulates independent layers in a premultiplied intermediate RenderTexture, then converts once to straight alpha for the actual foreground IMGUI image. A reusable CommandBuffer explicitly sets its render target, viewport, scissor and matrices; per-batch PropertyBlocks bind portrait/font textures. LateUpdate composes the frame and IMGUI only displays it. Both render targets, meshes, command buffer and material are reused and cleaned up. Rendering does not modify order cost, count, targets, attacks, damage, or pause rules.

Technical validation and limitations are recorded by the integrating task in `docs/QA.md`; this source note does not claim tests have run.
